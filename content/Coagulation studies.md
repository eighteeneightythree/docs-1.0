A routine coagulation test run by [Awanui labs](https://scg.labapps.nz/#/OTG/) includes the following component tests:
 - PT
 - APTT
 - Fibrinogen
 - +/- INR

# Prothrombin time
The prothrombin time is a measure of [[Coagulation cascade#extrinsic pathway|extrinsic pathway]] activity. It assesses tissue factor, factor VII, and the final common pathway.

# Activated partial thromboplastin time
The aPTT is a measure of the [[Coagulation cascade#intrinsic pathway|intrinsic pathway]] activity. It assesses factor XII, XI, IX, VIII, and the final common pathway. 

# Fibrinogen
Fibrinogen is synthesised primarily by the livers hepatocytes. It is a positive [[acute phase reactant]]. 

Fibrinogen concentration is typically tested for with immunoassays or the Clauss fibrinogen assay. 

 Hypofibrinogenaemia is either consumptive or due to a production deficit:
 - DIC
 - Liver disease
 - Transfusion

Hyperfibrinogenaemia may be an acute phase response:
- Inflammation
- Pregnancy
- Cancer
- Trauma

# International normalised ratio
The normalised ratio is the ratio of the patients prothrombin time to a control sample. It eliminates variation based on the PT assay. 

$INR = (PT_{test}/PT_{control})^{ISI}$

The INR is an surrogate measure of the [[Coagulation studies#Prothrombin time|PT]]. Its primary utility is in therapeutic monitoring of warfarin treatment.
