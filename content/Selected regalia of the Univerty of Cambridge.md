# B.A. gown
Back (on crease), 53" by 12".
Front, 53" by 24".

With standard widths this may be cut all in one width: **54"**

Yardage of black stuff: 137cm
# M.A. hood
Base square of 34.5" by 31", lined in lilac silk.
For both 69", plus 0.5" seam allowance: **70"**

Neck band 15" by 5", lined in white silk.
Plus 0.5" seam allowance: **6"**

Yardage of black stuff: 180cm
Yardage of lilac silk: 180cm

# Rough yardage (from basic calculations, maximum required)
**350cm** black stuff.
Not more than 200cm of silk.
