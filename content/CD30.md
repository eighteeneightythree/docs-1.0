[[Cluster of differentiation]] 30, also TNFSF8, a member of the TNR receptor superfamily. Characteristically associated with [anaplastic large cell lymphoma](T-cell%20lymphoma), and [[Hodgkin Lymphoma]].

It is the target of [brentuximab](Brentuximab).
