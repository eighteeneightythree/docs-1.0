A clinical laboratory conducts tests on specimens for routine testing, diagnostic workup, or treatment. 

A clinical laboratory practices applied science as opposed to [[pure science]].

Many clinical laboratories are attached to a hospital, however, private laboratories may be contracted to a hospital to provide services instead.
