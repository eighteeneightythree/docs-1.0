[[Cluster of differentiation]] 34 is a marker found on haematopoietic stem cells as well as vascular endothelial cells. 

It is often used to quantify the number of haematopoietic stem cells for use in [autologous stem cell transplant](Autologous%20stem%20cell%20transplant).
