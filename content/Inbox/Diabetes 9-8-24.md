Random glucose >11.1 in a symptomatic patient is diagnostic of DM.

Fasted glucose 6-7 indicative of pre-diabetes

Other causes of diabetes:

- MODY
- Gestational diabetes
- Acromegaly
- Cushing’s
- Phaeochromocytoma
- Glucagonoma

  

Latent autoimmune diabetes in adulthood - T1DM: Associated with lack of obesity, other autoimmunity.

T1DM: anti-GAD, anti-insulin, anti-ICA, anti-IA2

Correction Factor = 100/TDD(insulin)  
1u insulin will reduce BGL by the correction factor.  

  

T2DM:

Adipocyte FFA leakage causes macrophage recruitment and TNFa and IL-6 release, leading to insulin resistance, particularly in hepatocytes and skeletal myocytes.

  

Ghrelin regulates hunger - produced in the stomach.

GLP-1 (from small intestinal L-cells) signals satiety, as does PYY and CCK.
