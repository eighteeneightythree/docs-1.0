Working memory, sometimes also called "[[short-term memory]]" though they are not technically the same, is a cognitive system that can hold information temporarily. 

It is similar to a computer's [[RAM]] and stores working data while it is manipulated.

It is said that there is space for 7 ± 2 items in human working memory.
