Primary haemostasis apparently refers to the initial [[platelet adhesion, activation, and aggregation]] that occurs at a site of endothelial injury. Simply put, it is responsible for the formation of the platelet plug. Given the complex and synchronous haemostatic mechanisms, it is perhaps an anachronistic term.
