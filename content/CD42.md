[[Cluster of differentiation]] 42 or glycoprotein Ib is part of the von Willebrand Binding glycoprotein Ib-V-IX complex. 

Its components are GP1BA and GP1BB. The latter gene resides in region deleted in [[22q11.2 deletion syndrome]].
