A characteristic abnormality of leukocyte morphology, PHA is a congenital laminopathy that is most apparent in neutrophils and eosinophils. The cellular structure is also abnormal, with a course and lumpy cytoplasm.
It was first described by in 1928 by Karel Pelger, and identified as an inherited disorder in 1931.

# Psuedo-Pelger-Huët
The anomaly may also be acquired and sometimes develops due to haematological dyscrasias such as [[Acute myeloid lukaemia|AML]], [[Chronic myeloid lukaemia|CML]] or [[Myelodysplastic syndrome|MDS]]. The congenital form does not usually cause thrombocytopenia or leukopenias so a combined presentation warrants further investigation for MDS.
