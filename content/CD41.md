[[Cluster of differentiation]] 41, more commonly known as integrin alpha IIb (or glycoprotein IIb) is an integral membrane protein that forms part of the platelet fibrinogen receptor [[glycoprotein IIb/IIIa]].

It is the target of [[abciximab]].
