The Wolff-Chaikoff effect is inhibition of iodine uptake by the thyroid cells. 

The Plummer effect inhibits proteolysis of thyroglobulin. It is the effect that is utilised in treatment of thyrotoxicosis with Lugol’s iodine, a therapy called “Plummering”.
