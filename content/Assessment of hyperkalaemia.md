> [!caution] [[Hyperkalaemia]] is a potentially life threatening electrolyte disturbance and should be confirmed, investigated, and managed as soon as possible
# Required tests:
- [[Renal function test]]
- [[ECG]]

## Consider:
- [[Blood gas]] for rapid confirmation of serum potassium concentration

---
![[assessmentOfHyperkalaemia.svg]]
# Medications and dosage
- IV - Calcium gluconate 10%: **10-20mL (2.2-4.4mmol)**
- IV - Dextrose 50%: **50mL** + Actrapid insulin: **5units**
- Nebulised - Salbutamol **5mg**, repeated as necessary up to 20mg
