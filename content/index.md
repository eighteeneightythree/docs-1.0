In an effort to have my notes accessible from outside the iCloud ecosystem they will be intermittently backed up here. As the vault grows, so too will this repository...

When something makes its way into my notes, it will end up here when I run `npx quartz sync` from my touch bar. If something breaks, that is a shame. Feel free to submit a correction if you stumble across my contact details.

I write these notes for myself, some of them are brief, and most are unfinished. 

# On medicine
This is not a textbook, it is *my* study resource. If you should chose to study from it too, do so at your own peril. I make no promises about the authenticity of the information herein, most of it will be things I have forgotten over the course of a medical education. If something is wrong it is probably because I never understood it properly.

The notes are a reference guide, hopefully in time they can be my personal oxford handbook... and as fruitful and interesting as [Deranged Physiology](https://derangedphysiology.com).

*Notes are available by search:* ```cmd + K```
